Tropical zodiac, geocentric, apparent positions. Degrees and minutes are cut off, not rounded, so a body never shows in a sign before it gets there. Sidereal time is for Greenwich at the moment of each row. A station's exact time is in the tooltip on its mark.

The address bar keeps your range and settings, so you can bookmark or send a table as it is. Chiron is available from 1800 to 2399; outside those years the other bodies come from the Moshier theory built into the Swiss Ephemeris, which is a little less exact.
