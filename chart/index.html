Charts saved in this browser

    Tropical zodiac, geocentric, true node. Degrees and minutes are cut off, not rounded. Orbs: 8° with the Sun or Moon, 6° between planets, 5° to the angles, 3° for Chiron, the nodes and every quincunx.

    Local time becomes Universal Time through your browser's copy of the IANA time zone database, which carries each place's history of daylight saving and old local mean time. Check the line under each chart; if the zone history is wrong for a birth, type the offset yourself. With the time unknown, a chart is cast for noon with no houses or angles. A midpoint composite takes the nearer midpoint of each pair of planets; its houses are built from the composite Midheaven at the latitude midway between the places (the derived-Ascendant method). A Davison chart is a real chart for the moment halfway between the births, at the place halfway between them on the globe. With three or four people both use the average. Chiron is available from 1800 to 2399.
